The KUNETT Manufacturing Cycle

From 100% solid aerospace billets to automated 4-axis VMC machining, every millimeter is quality-controlled to ±0.05mm tolerances.

Step 01 • Metallurgy

Virgin 6063-T5 Billets

Zero recycled scrap. We cast and extrude exclusively with primary aerospace alloy for zero porosity and structural rigidity.

Step 02 • CNC Machining

4-Axis VMC Milling

High-speed CNC diamond tooling carves ergonomic curves, crisp bevels, and micro-precision screw center holes within ±0.05 mm accuracy.

Step 03 • Surface Chemistry

Multi-Tank Anodizing

A 15-micron sealed oxide layer molecularly fuses deep into the aluminium surface, providing permanent anti-fingerprint and corrosion resistance.

Step 04 • Quality Control

Individual Foam Packing

Every piece undergoes 100% scratch and thread inspection before being wrapped in protective foam sleeving for safe transit.
