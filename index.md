# KUNETT Hardware - Aluminium Handles & Handle Profile Manufacturer

> KUNETT Hardware (Harikrushna Enterprise) is a premier Indian manufacturer of aerospace-grade 6063-T5 solid aluminium architectural handles, continuous handle profiles (J-pull & G-profile), concealed wardrobe counsil handles, and cabinet knobs, located in Rajkot, Gujarat.

## Product Categories
- [Solid Aluminium Handles (200 Series)](https://kunetthardware.com/aluminium-handles.html): Heavy-duty solid pull handles for kitchen drawers and wardrobe shutters. Sizes: 96mm, 128mm, 160mm, 192mm, 224mm, 288mm, 320mm, 450mm, 600mm, 900mm, 1200mm.
- [Aluminium Handle Profile Systems (300 Series)](https://kunetthardware.com/aluminium-handle-profile.html): Continuous edge-mounted J-pull and Gola G-profiles, custom blade-cut up to 3 meters (10 feet).
- [Concealed Wardrobe Counsil Handles (400 Series)](https://kunetthardware.com/wardrobe-counsil-handles.html): Flush deep-recess concealed handles for 7ft to 9ft wardrobe panels.
- [Sliding Wardrobe Handles (450 Series)](https://kunetthardware.com/sliding-wardrobe-handles.html): Zero-clearance flush sliding door handles preventing shutter collisions.
- [Solid Aluminium Cabinet Knobs (500 Series)](https://kunetthardware.com/cabinet-knobs.html): Minimalist solid geometric knobs for vanities and dressers.
