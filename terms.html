Terms of Service & Wholesale Policy

Last updated: January 2026 • Harikrushna Enterprise (KUNETT Hardware), Rajkot, Gujarat

1. Legal Entity & Scope

KUNETT Hardware is a proprietary manufacturing brand of Harikrushna Enterprise, situated at Shop No. 5 & 6, Street No. 2, Maruti Industries, Rolex Rd, Kotharia, Rajkot - 360022, Gujarat, India.

These terms govern all commercial transactions, digital catalogue inquiries, wholesale agreements, bespoke profile cutting, and direct factory shipments executed through our website, WhatsApp channels, and direct factory communication.

2. B2B Wholesale Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ)

We cater to both individual home renovations and bulk commercial trade:

  • Homeowners & Architects: Single residential project quantities are accepted with direct factory invoicing. No minimum carton requirement for standard sizes.
  • Wholesale Distributors & Modular OEMs: Master cartons containing 50, 100, or 200 units (depending on handle length) receive full distributor tier pricing.
  • Continuous Handle Profiles (3-Meter Lengths): Minimum order of 5 pipes for secure transport wood-frame crating.

3. Dispatch & Logistics Timelines

All orders are dispatched directly from our Rajkot production facility:

  • Standard Catalog Finishes: Ready stock dispatches within 24 to 48 hours of order confirmation.
  • Custom Cut-to-Length Profiles: Specialized blade cutting and edge deburring orders dispatch within 4 to 6 business days.
  • Pan-India Road Transport: Reliable express road cargo to major hubs including Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, and Chennai within 3 to 5 transit days.
  • Worldwide Export: FOB Mundra Port or CIF air/sea cargo options with complete certificate of origin and export documentation.

4. 15-Micron Anodizing Finish Warranty

Every KUNETT aluminium handle and profile undergoes a rigorous 15-micron deep electrochemical anodizing treatment:

  • Warranty Scope: We guarantee our anodized surface against flaking, peeling, chipping, and fading under normal interior residential and commercial conditions.
  • Exclusions: Damage caused by abrasive chemical cleaners (bleach, harsh acids, caustic soda) or physical mechanical impact is excluded. Cleaning with a soft microfiber cloth and mild soapy water is recommended.

5. Inspection & Replacement Policy

We inspect every piece under high-intensity inspection lamps prior to individual bubble wrapping. If any component arrives with manufacturing defects or transit damage, notify us via WhatsApp at +91 99246 37640 with unboxing photographs within 7 days of delivery for immediate, no-cost replacement.

6. Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Harikrushna Enterprise respects your privacy:

  • Data Collected: Customer name, business name, WhatsApp/telephone number, city, and architectural drawings submitted for quotation.
  • Usage: Contact data is used exclusively to respond to your technical requests, generate custom price quotations, and arrange carrier dispatch.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or lease customer data to external marketing companies.
