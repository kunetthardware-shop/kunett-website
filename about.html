Engineering Architectural Elegance in 100% Solid Aluminium

KUNETT Hardware is the flagship architectural fittings brand of Harikrushna Enterprise. Located in Rajkot's industrial corridor, we manufacture solid 6063-T5 aluminium handles, continuous profile systems, and concealed hardware designed to last a lifetime.

Our Origins

From Precision Tooling to India's Trusted Hardware Partner

Founded in Rajkot by Sanjay Patel, Harikrushna Enterprise began with a focused vision: eliminate hollow, fragile zinc die-cast fittings and replace them with aerospace-grade, 100% solid virgin aluminium hardware.

Over the last decade, KUNETT has grown from an artisanal workshop into an advanced production ecosystem equipped with high-precision 5-axis CNC VMC milling centers, automatic hydraulic extrusion presses, and proprietary 15-micron electrochemical anodizing tanks.

Today, our architectural handles and J-pull / Gola profiles grace prestigious residential towers, modular designer kitchens, boutique hotels, and luxury villas across Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and overseas destinations.

Our Standards

Why Architects & Manufacturers Choose KUNETT

We control every micrometer from raw billet to sealed export carton.

01

Zero Scrap Metallurgy

We use solely primary Hindalco/Vedanta 6063-T5 virgin aluminium ingots. No remelted scrap or recycled impurities, guaranteeing zero pitting, brittle fractures, or corrosion.

02

5-Axis CNC VMC Precision

Every screw hole, chamfer, and grip groove is machined to ±0.05mm tolerance, ensuring swift, effortless installation for carpenters and modular assemblers.

03

Direct Factory Logistics

Direct dispatch from our Rajkot plant cuts out multi-tiered distributor margins. We fulfill bulk container shipments and custom residential projects with 24-48h dispatch.
