Direct Factory Engineering

4-Step Precision Manufacturing Process

How raw aerospace-grade 6063-T5 virgin billets are transformed into architectural hardware.

01

Virgin Billet Extrusion

1,800-ton hydraulic press extrusion utilizing strictly certified 6063-T5 virgin aluminium. Zero scrap remelt guarantees maximum grain density and zero structural porosity.

02

4-Axis CNC Micro-Milling

High-speed robotic CNC machining centers mill screw tapings, countersunk bevels, and tactile contours with an aerospace micro-tolerance of ±0.05 mm.

03

15µ Anodizing Studio

Multi-tank chemical etching followed by deep electrochemical anodizing creates a 15-micron impenetrable oxide shield resistant to sweat, corrosion, and kitchen steam.

04

100% Hand Inspection

Every piece undergoes 3-point micrometer inspection, surface gloss photometric verification, and is wrapped in anti-static micro-fleece sleeves before dispatch.
