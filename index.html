5 Red Flags When Sourcing Aluminium Handles from Rajkot

While Rajkot has world-class factories, hundreds of smaller unregulated workshops also operate in the market. As an OEM manufacturer or wholesale buyer, watch out for these critical red flags before placing bulk container or truckload orders:

⚠️ 1. Scrap Aluminium Ingot (Recycled Metal)

Many budget workshops melt down contaminated scrap aluminium to save costs. Recycled aluminium contains trapped iron impurities, resulting in brittle handles that snap under screw torque and develop microscopic pitting during anodizing. Always insist on 100% certified 6063-T5 virgin alloy billets like those used at KUNETT Hardware.

⚠️ 2. Cheap Spray Powder Coat Disguised as Anodizing

Real electrochemical anodizing fuses a diamond-hard aluminium oxide micron barrier into the metal. Cheap suppliers spray a 5-micron paint coating that looks identical on day 1, but begins peeling, chipping, and corroding when exposed to Indian kitchen spices, mustard oil vapors, or coastal humidity within 12 to 18 months.

⚠️ 3. Paper-Thin Profile Wall Thickness (< 0.8mm)

To cut raw aluminium costs, some extrusion workshops produce J-profiles and G-profiles with wall thicknesses of just 0.7mm to 0.8mm. When installed on 30kg - 40kg loaded tandem pantry drawers, these thin profiles flex and bend. High-spec architectural profiles must feature a minimum 1.2mm to 1.4mm structural wall thickness.

⚠️ 4. Razor-Sharp Cut Edges (Lack of CNC Chamfering)

Inexpensive profiles are chopped with dull mechanical circular saws without automated deburring or CNC edge-chamfering. End users and carpenters can easily cut their fingers on sharp burs. Ensure your supplier uses 100-tooth tungsten carbide tipped (TCT) precision cutting and chamfered edge treatments.

⚠️ 5. Inadequate Transit Packaging

Anodized matte black or gold finishes are vulnerable to friction scratches during 1,000+ km road transit to South or North India. Look for manufacturers that apply individual protective blue laser peeling film, individual foam sleeves, and heavy-duty 7-ply corrugated export cartons.

Why We Choose Aluminium Handles Over Steel Handles for a New Home

Discover why aluminium handles are better than steel for a lightweight design, durability, modern look, easy maintenance, and versatility.

When selecting aluminium handles for our new home decoration with doors, windows, cabinets, furniture, and other uses, the handle material matters more than you might think. A quality handle not only looks good but also needs to be sturdy, comfortable, and durable enough to be used for extended periods of time. Two metals that are commonly used include aluminium and steel, among others.

Another reason we prefer aluminium is the design and finishing freedom it offers. It can be shaped into different profiles and finished in different styles to match the look of modern furniture. With proper surface treatment, aluminium can also resist everyday wear and corrosion.

Steel has its own strengths and can be an excellent material for many applications. However, when you want a handle that feels light, looks modern, and offers design flexibility, aluminium becomes a natural choice. Ultimately, the right material depends on the application, quality, and finish—but for modern furniture, aluminium offers a combination that is hard to ignore.

Reasons for choosing Aluminium Handles over Steel Handles.

1. Lightweight :

One of the greatest strengths of aluminium is that it is lightweight. An aluminium handle is lighter than an iron handle, which makes it easier to fit in new houses. The great thing about aluminium is that it is not only light but also durable enough.

This makes it perfect for doors, windows, cabinets, furniture, and other hardware.

2. Better Resistance to Corrosion :

However, handles can be exposed to moisture and humidity and the changing environment. The rusting process will occur on steel when its protective coating gets damaged and when it gets exposed to moisture for a prolonged period.

The surface of aluminium forms a natural oxide coating which acts as a protective measure against corrosion. Aluminium handles can be made more resistant to the environment through an appropriate finishing method like anodising and powder coating.

Thus, aluminium can be considered a good choice for such places.

3. Modern and Attractive Appearance:

The appearance of a handle can make a noticeable difference to the overall look of a product. Aluminium provides a clean, modern appearance, which can be finished in all types of colours, designs, and surface treatments.

From raw material to finish colours like black, silver, rose gold, gold or other custom colours, aluminium provides manufacturers with new modern design possibilities. This flexibility allows aluminium handles to complement both traditional and contemporary designs.

4. Easy to Manufacture and Customise:

This metal is quite easy to process and can be moulded into any shape depending on the need. Different handle shapes can thus be manufactured based on their size, profile, shape, and finish

For manufacturers and companies, this feature is very important as handles can be designed according to the requirements of the product rather than following standard designs.

5. Easier Handling and Installation :

Due to the light nature of aluminium handles, it is easy to carry them around, position them, and fix them in place. It becomes especially helpful when a lot of handles are involved in building and manufacturing processes.

Being lighter helps to lower the weight of the final product.

6. Good Strength-to-Weight Ratio:

One of the myths is that aluminium gives up on strength.

Although steel is generally stronger in most of its basic mechanical properties than aluminium, strength is not the only characteristic that matters when making a decision about which handle to choose.

The aluminium metal offers a reasonable strength-to-weight ratio and can provide adequate strength for various furniture, architectural, and hardware applications at low weight of the finished item. Of course, it depends on the alloy, shape, dimensions, wall thickness, process, and usage of the handle.

Therefore, a properly designed aluminium handle can offer the required performance at low weight.

This is especially valuable for furniture and architectural hardware.

7. Comfortable to Handle :

Handles are used multiple times during their lifetime; thus, user comfort becomes very important in the design of handles.

Aluminium can be produced with smooth, round, ergonomic, or special-shaped surfaces. Therefore, it allows the making of comfortable handles to use.

The finish of a surface also affects the feel of a handle.

For instance, a brushed or matte finish may have a different tactile feeling compared to a highly polished finish.

However, the form of a handle still plays a very important role. Even the best material does not help if the design is wrong.

In this way, aluminum gives the possibility for producers to combine the characteristics of the material and its ergonomic design.

8. Suitable for Modern Furniture :

Use of aluminium handles is highly valuable in the furniture sector.

Furniture nowadays emphasizes simplicity in design with straight lines, minimalistic designs, and function-oriented hardware. Aluminium naturally suits this approach to furniture design.

Its light weight and variety of finishes suit residential and commercial furniture use.

For furniture producers, the possibility of purchasing aluminium profiles cut to desired lengths and processing them as per their needs is also an advantage.

9. Aluminium Performs Well in Humid Environments :

Moisture could also be a significant problem with regard to metal handles.

In the kitchen and bathroom, for instance, there could be relatively high levels of moisture. Handles that have been exposed outdoors could come into contact with rain and different weather elements.

Aluminium, being a material that naturally forms an oxide film, could be effective in providing resistance against corrosion in most of these environments.

This, however, does not mean that all aluminium handles could be suitable in all environments where there is corrosion.

For harsh environments, the right aluminium grade and surface coating should be selected.

10. Aluminium Offers Design Flexibility:

One of the major benefits of using aluminium in manufacturing includes the freedom in design.

This means that handles could be made using various lengths, profiles, sizes, and finish options.

Such versatility becomes especially useful in those cases when companies strive to develop their own product range.

Moreover, aluminium could be used together with various other materials to achieve different visual effects.

Aluminium vs Steel Handles: A Simple Comparison:

Both aluminium handles and steel handles have their own strengths and should be chosen based on the purpose of use. The main benefits of steel are high strength and good weight, while the main benefits of aluminium are lightweight design, anti-corrosion properties, stylish design, and versatility.

There are several key distinctions between aluminium and steel handles. First, aluminium handles weigh significantly less than steel handles because of the nature of the material. This can be extremely helpful for many types of furniture, such as cabinet drawers, sliding doors, wardrobes, and many other items where the unnecessary weight needs to be reduced. Second, one of the most important distinguishing features is its anti-corrosion ability. The thing is that aluminium develops a natural oxide film on its surface, which protects it from corrosion. Meanwhile, steel does not have this feature and may start to rust if there are oxygen and moisture around it. There are special coatings that can make steel resistant to corrosion, but if they are damaged and removed, the steel will start to rust. Therefore, aluminium handles are preferable for kitchens, bathrooms, windows, etc.

In what concerns strength, steel is better. The main application area of steel is those places where there is a need for strong and robust performance. However, not all furniture and architectural handles require the maximum possible strength of steel. A well-made aluminium handle will ensure necessary strength and will be much lighter than steel.

Apart from strength, the appearance and ability to make various shapes also matter in choosing a handle. One more benefit of aluminium handles is their modern design that can be made sleek and slim. Aluminium handles can be finished in several ways: anodized, brushed, polished, matte, powder coating, etc. There are also good finishing options for steel handles. At the same time, aluminium is especially suitable for modern designs based on specific profiles.

Finally, manufacturing flexibility should also be considered. Aluminium can be easily extruded in certain shapes and profiles and then be cut, drilled, machined and finished. Thus, aluminium is a good option for manufacturers with different requirements concerning the size and shape of handles. Steel is also very versatile. However, aluminium extrusion is especially good for long handle profiles.

Another aspect where the use of aluminium can prove to be advantageous is that of maintenance. Well-finished aluminium handles normally do not need much in terms of maintenance in the process of their use and they cannot form rust as easily as steel. The life of steel handles can also prove to be long if well-coated and maintained, but corrosion protection becomes more important.

In general, steel proves to be the most suitable material when strength is the main requirement, and aluminium is highly preferable in cases when weight, anti-corrosive properties, aesthetic value, ease of manipulation, and design are essential criteria.

In cases of furniture, kitchenware, wardrobes, cabinetry, doors, and windows, aluminium handles provide the best combination of all these characteristics, which makes aluminium a good choice for handle manufacturing.

Aluminium vs Steel Handles: Which One Is Better for Your Application?

Selecting the right handle involves more than just aesthetics. The material used to make the handle might influence the weight, strength, longevity, corrosion resistance, maintenance requirements, and general performance of the handle. Aluminium and steel are among the popular materials that can be used to manufacture handles. And they all have different strengths.

Which material should you go for?

It all depends on your needs. In case you require a handle that is light in weight, corrosion resistant, and attractive, the aluminium option will serve you well. But in case you are looking for maximum strength, rigidity, or weight, the steel alternative might be the best for your application.

Knowing the difference between the two materials will enable you to select the right handle for your needs.

Aluminium vs Steel: Understanding the Basic Difference.

Both aluminium and steel are strong and useful engineering materials, yet their characteristics are rather dissimilar.

First of all, the difference lies in weight. Aluminium has the density of 2.7 g/cm³, whereas that of steel equals 7.8–8.0 g/cm³. In simple words, a certain aluminium part weighs about one third of the same steel part.

This makes aluminium the perfect choice in case weight reduction is important.

Steel, on the other hand, usually offers greater strength and stiffness. This material is a good choice for those situations when the handles are likely to carry large loads, to receive heavy impact or be used in harsh working conditions.

In conclusion, there is no clear winner. It all depends on the requirements imposed on the handle.

Aluminium vs Steel Handle: Other Important Factors to Consider.

There is more to the comparison between the two metals' handles than their strength and weight alone. In addition to these aspects, there are other things that may need to be taken into account by manufacturers, furniture dealers, builders, interior decorators, and hardware merchants when choosing which type of handle to use.

1. Temperature and Heat Performance:

Temperature could have an impact on the handling of metal handles.

Aluminum is very good at conducting heat as compared to steel. Therefore, the aluminum handle is likely to change temperature according to the ambient surroundings faster. Steel takes time to change its temperature.

In regular indoor use in furniture, it does not make a big difference, but in outdoor conditions, in machinery surroundings, or where there is a likelihood of high temperatures, the environment should be taken into consideration.

It should always be based on the actual application and not just the material alone.

2. Handle Comfort:

The handle must be comfortable, since it might be used several times daily.

Aluminum is very light and can be produced in a variety of shapes and comfortable ergonomic designs, enabling manufacturers to produce handles that will be comfortable for the users.

Steel, similarly, can be made in comfortable shapes and finishes, while its increased density can provide a sense of solidity of the handle.

So, the comfort of use depends both on the material and the design of the handle.

3. Noise During Use:

Furthermore, the material as well as the design used to manufacture the handle also plays an important role in determining the noise produced.

A light handle made of aluminium will make different noises or produce a different feeling when it comes in contact with another metal than a heavy handle made of steel.

In case of furniture, cabinets, and drawers, it is important that they are installed properly rather than just using the right material.

4. Custom Handle Designs:

Some organizations may even have the need for handles that are not available in the commercial marketplace.

Aluminum is particularly suited for designing custom profile handles since it can be easily made in various profiles. These profiles can be designed based on the size requirement and cut to various sizes.

Branding or customized design

Steel is another material that can be customized based on various requirements.

5. Large-Volume Production:

It becomes even more vital in case when a company needs hundreds or even thousands of handles.

In addition to the lightweight of aluminium, its ability to be extruded could also be beneficial for mass production of the same profile.

Consistency is very important for the manufacturer of handles. Each one must have the right size, surface finish, holes for mounting, and look great.

This is why choosing an experienced handle manufacturer becomes as vital as choosing a material.

Conclusion:

It may not seem a big thing when selecting handles for your future house construction, but it really matters how comfortable you will be when using the furniture. Which colour is best for a wardrobe handle?

It depends on the wardrobe and interior. Black works well for strong contrast and modern interiors, while gold and rose gold can create a more premium and decorative appearance.

Can aluminium profile handles be used for other furniture?

Yes. Depending on the design, aluminium profile handles can be used for wardrobes, cabinets, drawers, modular kitchens, dressing units and other furniture applications.
