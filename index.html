Direct Factory Engineering

4-Step Precision Manufacturing Process

How raw aerospace-grade 6063-T5 virgin billets are transformed into architectural hardware in Rajkot.

01

Virgin Billet Extrusion

1,800-ton hydraulic press extrusion utilizing strictly certified 6063-T5 virgin aluminium. Zero scrap remelt guarantees maximum grain density and zero structural porosity.

02

4-Axis CNC Micro-Milling

High-speed robotic CNC machining centers mill screw tapings, countersunk bevels, and tactile contours with an aerospace micro-tolerance of ±0.05 mm.

03

15µ Anodizing Studio

Multi-tank chemical etching followed by deep electrochemical anodizing creates a 15-micron impenetrable oxide shield resistant to sweat, corrosion, and kitchen steam.

04

100% Hand Inspection

Every piece undergoes 3-point micrometer inspection, surface gloss photometric verification, and is wrapped in anti-static micro-fleece sleeves before dispatch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about sourcing architectural aluminium hardware directly from our Rajkot manufacturing facility.

What is the difference between aluminium handles and aluminium handle profile? ▾
Aluminium handles (200 Series) are traditional pull and bar handles mounted onto drawer faces with screws. Aluminium handle profiles (300 Series) are continuous edge-mounted profiles like J-pull and G-profiles that fit onto the shutter edge for modern, handle-less modular kitchens and wardrobes. KUNETT manufactures both solid aluminium handles and handle profiles at direct factory prices.
Can retail homeowners buy aluminium handles directly from KUNETT Hardware? ▾
Yes! Homeowners across India can order directly from KUNETT Hardware at factory prices. Whether you need handles for a single modular kitchen or an entire home renovation, we provide custom sizes, color matching, and safe doorstep delivery.
What is the best handle style for modern modular kitchens? ▾
For modern minimalist kitchens, KUNETT 300 Series edge profiles (J-profile and G-profile) and 200 Series solid aluminium pull handles in Matte Black or Gold are the most popular choices. They offer a clean handle-less aesthetic and are easy to maintain.
Which handles are recommended for tall wardrobe sliding doors? ▾
For sliding wardrobe doors, KUNETT 450 Series Sliding Counsils and 400 Series Counsil Handles are ideal. Their flush-mount, zero-clearance design prevents doors from colliding when sliding open and closed.
What finishes are available for KUNETT aluminium handles and profiles? ▾
KUNETT handles come in 8 signature luxury finishes: Matte Black, Rose Gold (R Gold), Matte Gold, Champagne, SS Matt (Stainless Steel Finish), Antique Brass, CP (Chrome Plated), and Architectural Grey. All finishes are electrochemically anodized to prevent peeling and fading.
Are aluminium handles more durable than zinc alloy or brass handles? ▾
Yes. 100% solid aerospace-grade virgin aluminium handles will never rust, warp, or crack. Unlike electroplated zinc which tarnishes and bubbles over time, KUNETT's electrochemical anodizing bonds color deep into the metal structure for decades of durability.
Who is the manufacturer behind KUNETT Hardware? ▾
Harikrushna Enterprise under the brand KUNETT Hardware is a premier manufacturer and global exporter founded by Sanjay Patel in Rajkot, Gujarat, India with over 28 years of manufacturing excellence supplying 100+ wholesale partners and thousands of homes.
Do you provide custom handle lengths for bespoke interior projects? ▾
Yes, we offer precision blade cutting and VMC machining to provide custom profile lengths from 6 inches up to 3 meters for full-height wardrobes and custom kitchen cabinetry.
Does KUNETT Hardware export aluminium handles globally to USA and Germany? ▾
Yes, KUNETT Hardware exports architectural aluminium hardware worldwide, with key export markets including the United States, Germany, the UK, Europe, and Middle Eastern countries with international wholesale packaging.
