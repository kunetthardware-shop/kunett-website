The KUNETT Manufacturing Cycle

From 100% solid aerospace billets to automated 4-axis VMC machining, every millimeter is quality-controlled to ±0.05mm tolerances.

Step 01 • Metallurgy

Virgin 6063-T5 Billets

Zero recycled scrap metal. We cast and extrude exclusively with primary aerospace-grade aluminium alloy for zero porosity and structural rigidity.

Step 02 • CNC Machining

4-Axis VMC Milling

High-speed CNC diamond tooling carves ergonomic curves, crisp bevels, and micro-precision screw center holes within ±0.05 mm accuracy.

Step 03 • Surface Chemistry

Multi-Tank Anodizing

A 15-micron sealed oxide layer molecularly fuses deep into the aluminium surface, providing permanent anti-fingerprint and corrosion resistance.

Step 04 • Quality Control

Individual Foam Packing

Every single piece undergoes a 100% scratch and thread inspection before being wrapped in protective foam sleeving for safe transit.

Architectural Hardware Standards • Rajkot

KUNETT Architectural Hardware is an ISO-certified manufacturer specializing in high-grade 6063-T5 solid aluminium cabinet handles, continuous J-pull profiles, Gola profiles, concealed flush handles, and precision-turned knobs. Based in Rajkot, Gujarat—India's premier architectural hardware manufacturing hub—we supply luxury residential developments, commercial projects, interior contracting firms, and wholesale hardware distributors worldwide.

Unlike commodity zinc die-cast hardware that suffers from internal air voids, porosity, and flaking electroplating, KUNETT products are extruded exclusively from primary virgin billets. Our 15-micron electrochemical anodizing process molecularly bonds color into the aluminium crystal matrix, providing permanent resistance to kitchen cooking steam, skin acids, and coastal humidity.
