Frequently Asked Questions About Cabinet Knobs

Everything you need to know about specifications, installation, finishes, and ordering from KUNETT.

Q. What makes KUNETT solid aluminium knobs unique?

Each knob in our 500 Series is CNC-milled from a solid aerospace billet of aluminium. The weight, tactile feel, chamfered bevels, and micro-knurled grip provide an unmistakably luxurious sensation.

Q. Where are these knobs best installed?

They are ideal for bathroom vanities, bedroom nightstands, office credenzas, custom dressers, and accent kitchen drawers.
